
# My Dashboard - P301

## What is this?
An operational dashboard for a sustainability manager that aims to update the company’s sustainability strategy and goals across the FY. The company partners with global companies that are following the ESG goals. It also works on the side with ONGs that partner community organizations that are looking to improve eco education in schools/communities, on how to reduce their waste and carbon emissions and the impact industries and people have on sustainability. In the future even the global companies could develop programs for these ONGs communities.

## Data
The main metrics to track in this dashboard is identifying overall risks and opportunities from the relationship of all partners (global companies). Then the user would have a way to select which partner data they would like to analyze and get Insights on: how they’re doing in their internal ESG Progress and how we could support their goals, align their strategy in a successful path, or even send alerts/notifications of their monthly summary and next recommended personalized actions for them.

This dashboard would also have a section with real world data, for the manager to understand what’s happening with climate change metrics in real time, current and foresight weather, global natural events, and how that is affecting current and future goals across the general company strategy and the community Impact, and recommended actions. These actions are personalized and could be linked to infernal activities. e.g. include these month’s results to main strategy roadmap FY26. ## Interactions
- Filters will apply to all the data across the board. For example, the filtering made for month/year will apply to both overview and dive deep and user will be able to change it at any screen, the data will update automatically for all content. 
- All chart visualizations will have a way to interact with the data by hovering or clicking to see progress movement across data.

## Style
- Make the selected, hover, and pressing state for buttons, dropdowns and tab selectors.
- Light Mode by default - with toggle to switch to dark, this will be integrated in the top nav bar
- Clean, minimal, Lots of whitespace and divisions per section.
- Mobile and table responsive - cards Stack on smaller screens and resize for tablet Mode. 
- Recommend a color palette related to ONGs, something with Blue as the main brand color, include Blue and gray in the palette.

## Tech
